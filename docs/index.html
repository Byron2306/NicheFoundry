01 / The operating system

One foundry.
Every stage.

Most AI video tools automate one attractive fragment. NicheFoundry governs the whole chain, preserving the decisions and evidence that made the final programme possible.

05 / Provenance as architecture

The final video is not the only thing that matters.

NicheFoundry keeps research, claims, scripts, visuals, audio, render state, compliance decisions, and publishing state separate. You can inspect how the programme came to be, not merely whether an MP4 appeared.

01Source ledger

Facts remain attached to the evidence that supports them.

02Decision trail

Creative choices, overrides, and critic reports stay visible.

03Asset provenance

Generated, licensed, captured, and original visuals are distinguished.

The system may prepare a release. A person still authorises it.
