# Editorial Review Guide

## Recommended local workflow

1. Generate or select an episode.
2. Open **Review Room and Final Sign-off**.
3. Select a reviewer identity.
4. Build the review queue.
5. Assign each task to its appropriate role.
6. Review research, script, visuals, and performance planning.
7. Record editorial approval.
8. Build and review audio.
9. Record audio approval.
10. Build a proxy, correct individual scenes, and then build the final render.
11. Leave scene- and timestamp-bound comments while watching.
12. Resolve every blocker.
13. Record render approval.
14. Capture a final version snapshot.
15. Compare it with the earlier editorial checkpoint.
16. Record final sign-off.

## Review roles

### Researcher

Assemble and annotate the connector packet, source hierarchy, freshness evidence, and research notes. This task is advisory, so a solo operator can continue when the intake pass is not assigned, but it remains visible in the audit trail.

### Fact Checker

Review source independence, freshness, conflict status, exact claim support, and uncertainty language.

### Writer / Script Editor

Review hook honesty, narrative order, spoken language, repetition, timing, and whether the conclusion delivers the channel promise.

### Visual Editor

Review composition, text safety, evidence overlays, asset rights, thumbnail promise, and anti-template similarity.

### Audio Editor

Review host direction, pronunciation, pacing, loudness, transitions, music ducking, and scene timing.

### Channel Owner

Watch the final programme end to end. Confirm that the programme is useful, coherent, correctly branded, and faithful to the approved premise.

### Publisher

Confirm audience classification, synthetic-media disclosure, captions, thumbnail, rights, and release metadata before final sign-off.

## Blockers

Use `blocker` only for an issue that must prevent approval, such as:

- unsupported factual claim
- unresolved source conflict
- rights uncertainty
- incorrect pronunciation of a key term
- missing or misleading caption
- audio clipping or sync failure
- false thumbnail promise
- incorrect audience or disclosure setting

Use `suggestion` for improvements that do not make the current version unsafe or misleading.

## Version snapshots

Capture snapshots at minimum:

- after the editorial packet is approved
- after audio approval
- after final render approval
- immediately before final sign-off

A zero-change comparison between the final render approval and final sign-off is a strong release-control signal.
